Your campaign

Progress saves in this browser as you go. Sign in with Google from the account button at the top right to sync it across your devices; guest progress on this browser is carried into a new account.

To bring in progress from another copy of the tracker, choose Save progress backup there, then Restore backup below. Saving a backup before importing or replacing progress is still a good idea.

Check individual objectives as you finish them. After returning to Speranza and turning in a quest, mark the quest completed. This unlocks the next quests in the tracker. If you mark a quest completed by accident, use “Undo last quest” on Next Raid to reopen it and refresh the recommended deployment. Your individual objective checks are kept. On Next Raid, completed cards stay visible and desaturated until you press “Finish this deployment”, even if every quest is checked. Uncheck a completed card to restore its color and reopen it. Finish after each raid to rebuild the recommendation from quests you have actually turned in and your remaining map objectives. Unfinished quests keep their checks and can appear on a later brief; you may be sent to the same map again. Quest checkboxes never advance the deployment. To start a brand new campaign, choose “Reset campaign” and confirm with “Proceed”.

Tap an underlined supply name to open item intel with ARC loot sources, suggested search areas, and reference links. Use the X, Escape, or click outside to close it. Underlined location objectives open a marked map overlay without leaving the tracker. Use the X, Escape, or click outside to close it. The objective checkbox stays independent. Markers identify quest areas; follow the objective text for floors and indoor interactions. Some objectives allow several valid locations.

The adaptive route compares map combinations and follow-up unlocks to reduce the remaining raid count. It is a planning heuristic, not a proven mathematical minimum. Future deployments are read-only projections that assume the earlier brief’s objectives and turn-ins are completed. Only genuinely unlocked quests appear in the current recommendation.

The “Look out for” panel on Next Raid checks the next three projected deployments and names items to save for their quests. It reserves current quest needs first, subtracts your remaining collected supplies, and flags usable quest rewards instead of asking you to collect them twice. Supplies tracks items you can collect in advance across this campaign, with labels for trader turn-ins and raid use. Bold “Bring into raid” notices on quest cards list what to pack, including free gear provided when accepting a quest. Keep that gear until you finish the relevant objective; it is excluded from collection totals. Collection targets subtract usable rewards received earlier in the projected raid route; each item names its reward source. The full quest requirements remain visible. Quest-specific pickups are tracked through their quest objectives. Supplies shows your current stash quantity. Completing a quest subtracts the recorded turn-in items and consumable raid supplies; reusable carry-in gear stays in your stash. Undo restores the quantities actually deducted. Items disappear from Supplies when no unfinished quest needs them and return if a matching quest is reopened. Include rewards in your stash only after receiving and keeping them. If you sell, recycle or lose a reserved reward, collect a replacement and adjust your stash quantity.
