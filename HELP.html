Your progress

Progress always saves in this browser first. If you sign in with Google (account button, top right), it also syncs to your account and follows you to any device where you sign in. Changes made on one device appear on the others within a few seconds while both are open.

The first time you sign in, any guest progress on that browser is carried into your new account. If your account already has progress, the account copy is loaded instead (unless this browser has newer unsynced changes).

The tracker guide also has Save progress backup and Restore backup for a separate JSON copy on disk.

Privacy

Signing in stores your Google account's name, email and user ID with Firebase Authentication, and your tracker progress in a Firestore document that only your account can read or write. Nothing else is collected. Signing out returns this browser to its guest progress.

What works

Next Raid, adaptive planning, Quest Log, Supplies, item details, map close-ups and markers, reference photos, language switching, undo, reset, backup/import and cloud sync.
